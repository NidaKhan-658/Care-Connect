# Project Charter: CareConnect Feature Enhancement

## Business Problem
Patients frequently miss follow-up appointments and medication doses due to 
inconsistent reminder systems and lack of a centralized way to communicate 
with care providers. This leads to poor health outcomes and increased 
readmission rates.

## Project Goals
1. Reduce appointment no-show rate by improving reminder and scheduling flow
2. Improve medication adherence through automated tracking and reminders
3. Enable secure, HIPAA-compliant messaging between patients and providers

## Success Metrics (KPIs)
- Reduce no-show rate by 15% within 3 months of launch
- Increase medication adherence logging by 25%
- Achieve 80% patient satisfaction score on new messaging feature

## Scope
**In scope:** Appointment reminders, medication adherence tracking, 
secure provider messaging (mobile + web)

**Out of scope:** Billing integration, insurance verification, 
telehealth video calls (future phase)

## Key Stakeholders
- Patients (end users)
- Care coordinators
- Providers/physicians
- Compliance/Legal team
- IT/Engineering team

## Assumptions & Constraints
- Must comply with HIPAA regulations
- Existing patient database will be reused (no new patient onboarding flow)
- Feature must work on both iOS and Android

- Sprint 0 (Planning): Week 1
- Sprint 1-2 (Build): Week 2-5
- UAT & Launch: Week 6
