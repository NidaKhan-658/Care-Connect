# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Interest Level | Influence Level | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patients | High | Medium | End users of the feature |
| Care Coordinators | High | High | Daily users, workflow impact |
| Providers/Physicians | Medium | High | Approve clinical workflow changes |
| Compliance/Legal | Medium | High | Must approve HIPAA compliance |
| IT/Engineering | High | Medium | Build and maintain the feature |
| Hospital Leadership | Low | High | Budget approval, sponsor |
