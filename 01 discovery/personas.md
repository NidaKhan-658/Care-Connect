# User Personas

## Persona 1: Margaret, 68 — Retired, Low Tech Literacy
- **Goals:** Wants simple reminders she won't miss; prefers phone calls or SMS over apps
- **Frustrations:** Finds app notifications confusing; forgets to check email
- **Needs from CareConnect:** Large text, SMS-based reminders, minimal taps

## Persona 2: James, 34 — Working Parent, Tech-Comfortable
- **Goals:** Wants to manage his and his kids' appointments in one place
- **Frustrations:** No time to call the clinic during work hours; missed reminders 
  because they don't sync with his calendar
- **Needs from CareConnect:** Calendar sync, push notifications, ability to manage 
  multiple family profiles

## Persona 3: Priya, 41 — Care Coordinator
- **Goals:** Wants to see which patients are at risk of missing appointments or 
  doses so she can intervene early
- **Frustrations:** Currently uses spreadsheets to track patient follow-ups manually
- **Needs from CareConnect:** A dashboard view of adherence/no-show trends across 
  her patient list
