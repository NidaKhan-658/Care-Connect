# Success Metrics & Outcome Measurement

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 1. Appointment No-Show Rate
- **Target:** Reduce no-show rate by 15% within 3 months of launch
- **How measured:** (Missed appointments / Total scheduled appointments) x 100, 
  tracked weekly and compared to pre-launch baseline
- **Baseline benchmark:** Industry studies show SMS appointment reminders 
  typically reduce no-show rates by 20-30%, so a 15% target is a conservative, 
  achievable goal for a first release

### 2. Medication Adherence Logging Rate
- **Target:** Increase adherence logging by 25% compared to manual tracking methods
- **How measured:** (Doses logged as "TAKEN" / Total scheduled doses) x 100, 
  tracked per patient and in aggregate

### 3. Patient Satisfaction with Messaging Feature
- **Target:** 80% satisfaction score
- **How measured:** In-app survey sent to patients after their first use of 
  the secure messaging feature (5-point scale, target average of 4.0+)

### 4. Care Coordinator Efficiency
- **Target:** Reduce time spent manually tracking patient follow-ups by 30%
- **How measured:** Time-tracking survey with care coordinators, pre- and 
  post-launch, comparing hours spent on manual outreach

## Measurement Cadence
- Weekly: No-show rate, adherence logging rate (automated dashboard)
- Monthly: Patient satisfaction survey results, care coordinator feedback
- Quarterly: Full KPI review against original charter goals, presented to 
  hospital leadership sponsor

## What Happens If Targets Are Missed
- If no-show rate improvement falls below 10% after month 1, the team will 
  investigate reminder timing (e.g., testing 48-hour vs. 24-hour reminder windows)
- If adherence logging is low, this may indicate the SMS reply flow is unclear — 
  triggering a UX review of the reminder message copy
